01 / TABLE EGGS

Everyday goodness in every egg.

Eggs are a versatile food enjoyed in homes, restaurants, bakeries and other food businesses. They can be prepared in many ways and used in a wide range of meals.

Why eggs?

  • Provide high-quality protein
  • Contain vitamin B12
  • Contain choline
  • Useful for everyday cooking and baking

Ways to enjoy them

Boil, fry, scramble or use eggs in baking, sauces and other recipes. Keep eggs handled and stored appropriately to maintain food quality and safety.
