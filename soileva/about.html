ABOUT SOILEVA

Growing food.
Growing with nature.

We are a farming and food business committed to growing and supplying food with care for people, the land and the communities we serve.

OUR STORY

Rooted in farming.
Focused on food.

For over a decade, Soileva has been guided by a simple belief: good farming should help put good food on people's tables.

We work across farming and food production, growing crops and raising poultry as part of our contribution to the food supply chain.

Our work continues to grow, shaped by the needs of farmers, customers and the communities connected to the food we produce.

WHAT WE DO

Different activities.
One purpose: food.

Our farming activities cover different parts of food production, from growing crops to raising poultry.

Vegetables

We cultivate fresh vegetables as part of our work to grow food for everyday needs.

Poultry

We raise poultry and produce food such as eggs for consumers and the wider food market.

Rice

We also cultivate long-grain rice as part of our crop farming activities.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Care for the land.
Care for people.

Farming is about more than what we harvest today. It is also about how we care for the land and the resources that make food production possible.

At Soileva, we believe in using resources thoughtfully, caring for the soil and considering the people and communities connected to our work.

We recognise that responsible farming is an ongoing effort, and we want our growth to contribute positively to the land and the people we serve.
