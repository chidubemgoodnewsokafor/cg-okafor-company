01 / CABBAGE

Fresh, crisp cabbage.

Cabbage is a leafy vegetable enjoyed raw or cooked. Its mild flavour and crisp texture make it suitable for salads, slaws, stir-fries, soups and traditional meals.

Nutritional benefits

  • Provides vitamin C
  • Contains dietary fibre
  • Provides vitamin K
  • Contains protective plant compounds

How to use it

Slice it into salads, lightly cook it with other vegetables, or add it to soups and stews. Wash thoroughly before use.

02 / RED CABBAGE

Bold colour. Valuable nutrition.

Red cabbage is recognised by its deep purple-red leaves and crisp texture. Its distinctive colour comes largely from anthocyanins, plant pigments with antioxidant activity. It brings colour, crunch and variety to meals.

Nutritional benefits

  • Contains anthocyanins with antioxidant activity
  • Provides vitamin C
  • Contains dietary fibre
  • Provides vitamin K

How to use it

Shred it into fresh salads and slaws, combine it with other vegetables, or lightly cook it as a colourful side dish. Wash thoroughly before use.

03 / CUCUMBER

Cool, crisp cucumber.

Cucumber is a refreshing, water-rich vegetable commonly enjoyed raw. Its crisp texture works well in salads, sandwiches, snacks and chilled dishes.

  • Provides some vitamin K

12 / CANTALOUPE

Sweet, fragrant and naturally refreshing.

Cantaloupe is a sweet, aromatic melon recognised by its netted outer skin and juicy orange flesh. It is enjoyed as a fresh fruit, especially when chilled, and makes a refreshing addition to fruit bowls, breakfast tables and healthy snacks.

Nutritional benefits

  • Rich in beta-carotene, which the body converts to vitamin A
  • Provides vitamin C
  • High water content supports hydration
  • Contains potassium and other essential nutrients

How to enjoy it

Wash the outside thoroughly before cutting. Remove the seeds and serve the orange flesh in slices or cubes. Add it to fruit salads, breakfast bowls and smoothies, or enjoy it chilled as a refreshing snack. Refrigerate cut cantaloupe and use clean utensils when serving.
