01 ABOUT AGRISCIENCE

Practical solutions.
Better agricultural outcomes.

Okafor Agriscience is the agricultural science business of C.G. Okafor Co. We focus on solutions that address practical needs across crop production and animal health.

Our focus areas span crop protection, plant nutrition, seeds and animal health. We aim to connect farmers with relevant products and solutions that support agricultural productivity and the wider food supply.
