About Okafor Agriscience

Practical science for the needs of farming.

Farming brings together many connected challenges. Crops must be protected from weeds, pests and diseases. Plants need the right nutrition. Farmers need suitable seeds, while livestock producers need solutions that support animal health.

Okafor Agriscience focuses on these essential needs through science-based agricultural products and solutions. Our aim is to help farmers make informed decisions and access options suited to their production needs.

As the agricultural science business of C.G. Okafor Co., Okafor Agriscience contributes to our wider commitment to better farming and a stronger food supply.
