OUR AGRISCIENCE BUSINESS

Helping farmers meet the challenges of growing food.

Agriculture depends on the decisions farmers make every day. The health of a crop, the nutrients available to plants, the quality of seed and the health of livestock all influence productivity and the food that reaches people.

Through Okafor Agriscience, C.G. Okafor Co. brings together agricultural products, knowledge and practical solutions designed around the needs of farmers and food producers.
