Frequently asked
questions.

Some quick answers to help you get started.

How do I find the products I need?

Browse our product categories or use the online store to search for specific agricultural products. You can also contact our team for assistance.

What do IR and other resistance terms mean when choosing seeds?

Seed varieties may carry different resistance or tolerance characteristics. Understanding these terms can help you select varieties suited to your crop and farming conditions.

How do I choose the right dosage for my flock?

The appropriate dosage depends on the product, animal type, age, weight and intended use. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions or consult a qualified animal health professional.

Do you deliver to my location?

We work to make our products accessible to customers in different locations. Contact us to confirm delivery availability and arrangements for your area.

Can I buy now and pay later?

Payment arrangements may vary depending on the product and applicable terms. Please contact our team to find out whether this option is available.
