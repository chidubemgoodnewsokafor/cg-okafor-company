FARM DESIGN & BUILDING

The right environment starts with the design.

Poultry housing needs to work for the birds, the equipment and the people managing the farm. We approach farm design with the entire operation in mind.

01

Egg Layer

Farm environments designed around the requirements of laying birds, daily workflow, equipment integration and efficient egg production.

Good layer-house design considers ventilation, lighting, feeding, watering, manure handling, egg collection and access for workers.

02

Broiler

Housing designed around broiler production, environmental management, feeding, watering and practical farm operations.

Broiler housing requires careful attention to air movement, temperature, stocking, feeding, watering, litter and monitoring.

AUTOMATION / SOLUTIONS

Connecting the systems that keep the farm moving.

Technology can reduce unnecessary manual work, improve consistency and give farmers better visibility into what is happening inside the poultry house.

01

Feeding & Watering

Consistent access to feed and water is fundamental to a well-managed poultry operation.

Feeding and watering systems can help distribute resources more consistently, reduce unnecessary manual work and give farmers greater control over daily operations.

02

Manure Management

Better manure handling supports cleaner working environments and more manageable farm operations.

Poor waste management can contribute to unpleasant conditions, moisture problems and additional workload. Proper systems help farmers manage these challenges more effectively.

03

Egg Collection

Efficient egg handling can reduce unnecessary manual work and support smoother daily operations.

Egg collection systems can be integrated into the wider layer operation so that collection, handling and movement are easier to manage.

04

Brooding & Debeaking

Early-stage poultry management requires carefully controlled conditions and practical systems.

Brooding environments need appropriate temperature, ventilation, lighting, feeding and watering arrangements. Specific debeaking practices should always follow appropriate welfare standards and professional guidance.

05

Climate Control / Lighting

Environmental conditions inside the poultry house can have a major influence on bird comfort and farm management.

Ventilation, temperature, humidity and lighting can be monitored and managed as part of a connected farm environment.

06

Monitoring

Better information helps farmers see what is happening inside the farm and respond more quickly.

Monitoring can bring together information about important environmental and operational conditions, helping farmers make better-informed decisions.

POULTRY FARM CHALLENGES

Good technology starts with understanding the problem.

The purpose of farm technology is not simply to install equipment. It is to solve real problems that farmers experience every day.

01

Poor Air Quality

Poor ventilation and environmental control can contribute to humidity, uncomfortable conditions and wet litter.

02

Uneven Feeding

Uneven feed distribution or poor access can create inconsistent intake and unnecessary feed waste.

03

Delayed Information

When important changes inside the house are not noticed early, small problems can become more difficult to manage.

04

Poor House Design

The wrong layout can make ventilation, cleaning, movement, equipment operation and daily management more difficult.
