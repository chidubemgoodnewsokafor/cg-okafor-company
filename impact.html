FARMERS BY OKAFOR

The people behind our impact.

Every farmer has a story. There are challenges that come with each farming season, decisions that affect production and opportunities to do things better.

Through practical agricultural solutions and support, we work alongside farmers to help them overcome challenges and make progress on their farms.

Mr. Kingsley

Onitsha, Anambra State

Broiler Farmer
“

One of the challenges in broiler farming is keeping production consistent while managing the cost of raising the birds. I needed dependable farm inputs and practical support to improve the way I manage my flock. The support from Okafor helped me make better-informed decisions on the farm and improve my approach to broiler production. It has made managing the birds more organised and given me greater confidence in my production process.

Layer Farm
“

With a layer farm, one challenge is maintaining healthy birds and keeping egg production consistent. Poor decisions around feeding and farm management can affect the whole operation. The support and solutions we received from Okafor helped us pay closer attention to our birds' needs and improve our day-to-day management. We are better equipped to manage the flock and work towards more consistent production.
