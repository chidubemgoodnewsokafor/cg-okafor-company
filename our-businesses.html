OUR BUSINESSES

Building a better
food system.

Our businesses bring together agriculture, technology and food to support farmers, strengthen production and help create a more reliable food supply.

WHAT WE DO

From the farm
to the food system.

C.G. Okafor Co. operates across interconnected areas of agriculture and food production. We develop solutions for farmers, apply technology to production, produce and source food, and create value from agricultural output.

Together, our businesses help us understand the needs of the people who produce food and the people who depend on it.
