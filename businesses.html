OUR BUSINESSES

From better farming
to food for all.

Our businesses connect agricultural science, farm technology, farming and food. Together, they support the people who produce our food and help create practical solutions across the food and agricultural value chain.

WHAT WE DO

Different capabilities.
A shared purpose.

C.G. Okafor Co. is a food and agricultural solutions and technology company. Our work spans the practical needs of farmers, the technologies that support agricultural production, and the sourcing, production and development of food.

Each business has its own area of focus. Together, they reflect our commitment to improving farming, strengthening food supply and creating value throughout the agricultural and food system.
