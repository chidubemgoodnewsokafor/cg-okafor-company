WHAT WE DO

Better farming.
Food for all.

C.G. Okafor Co. is a food and agricultural solutions and technology company focused on creating practical value across the agricultural and food supply chain.

Our businesses approach agriculture from different but complementary directions. We support farming through science and technology, participate in food production and processing, and develop commercial activities that connect agricultural output with market opportunities.

Together, these activities reflect our commitment to productive farming, dependable food supply and long-term agricultural development.

ONE CONNECTED APPROACH

From agricultural knowledge to food on the table.

Agriculture creates opportunities at every stage, from the inputs and technology farmers need to the produce they grow and the food markets ultimately receive. Our businesses operate across complementary parts of this journey.

01. Enable

Agricultural science and farm technology provide products, knowledge and practical capabilities that support farming operations.

02. Produce

Commercial farming and agricultural production contribute to the supply of crops, livestock products and other agricultural commodities.

03. Connect

Food sourcing, processing and trading help connect agricultural output with markets, businesses and consumers.

OKAFOR FOODS

Connecting agricultural produce with food markets.

Food systems depend on more than production alone. Sourcing, handling, processing and distribution all play important roles in making agricultural products available to people and businesses.

Our food activities are focused on agricultural commodities, poultry and eggs, edible oils, fruit and vegetables, seasonings and other value-added food opportunities.
